FAQ

Answers before we start.

Everything you need to know before requesting a quote.

What type of videos do you edit?

I edit short-form content for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts, as well as cinematic edits, highlight videos, promos and motion-graphics projects. If your idea does not fit neatly into one of those categories, you can still send through the details and I’ll let you know whether it is something I can help with.

Will Ollie personally edit my video?

Yes. If I accept your project, I will be the person editing it and communicating with you throughout the process.

I consider every enquiry fairly, but I may decline a project if it is outside my current skills, availability, values or the type of work I am comfortable creating. If I turn down an idea, I will give you a respectful, clear reason where appropriate rather than wasting your time or promising something I cannot deliver well.

My editing workflow may use AI-assisted features built into editing software—for example, to support tasks such as captions, audio cleanup, masking or workflow organisation. I remain responsible for the creative decisions, final edit and quality of the work.

Are you a professional editor?

I am an independent teenage video editor and motion designer, not a large professional agency or formally trained editor. I take every project seriously and will put genuine effort into creating the strongest result I can.

My experience and results may differ from editors with years of industry training or larger production teams. I will always aim to be clear about what I can deliver, communicate honestly and only take on work that I believe I can complete to a good standard.

Can you match a style or reference video?

Yes. Send links to videos, creators or examples you like when you request a quote. I can use them as a creative reference for pacing, captions, colour, sound design, transitions, motion graphics and overall feel—while making sure your video still fits your own content and audience.

How much does video editing cost?

Each project is quoted individually. Cost can depend on the raw-footage length, final video length, editing style, captions, motion graphics, deadline, number of videos needed and how detailed the project is.

Send through some details using the quote form and I’ll provide a clear estimate before any editing begins.

How long will my edit take?

Turnaround time depends on the project scope and my current availability. Short-form videos will normally take less time than longer edits or projects with detailed motion graphics.

If you have a deadline, include it in your quote request. I will confirm whether I can meet it before accepting the project.

How do I send my footage?

Once we have agreed on the project, you can send your footage using a Google Drive, iCloud Drive or OneDrive sharing link. Please make sure the link permissions allow me to view or download the files.

If you have a lot of clips, placing them in clearly named folders—such as Footage, Music, Logo, References and Brief—will make the editing process smoother.

Are revisions included?

A reasonable number of revision rounds can be included in your quote, giving you an opportunity to provide feedback after seeing the first version.

Major changes outside the original brief—such as changing the video’s purpose, using completely new footage or requesting a different style—may need an updated quote. I will always discuss this with you before doing extra work.

Do you create captions and motion graphics?

Yes. I can add captions, animated text, basic motion graphics, visual effects, sound design and other elements that help content feel more engaging and ready for its intended platform.

If you have a particular style in mind, include an example link in your quote request.

What will you not edit?

I will not create or edit content involving:

  • Sexual content, nudity, pornography, sexualised content involving anyone under 18 or exploitative material
  • Graphic violence, real violence, gore, cruelty or content intended to shock or disturb people
  • Hate speech, harassment, bullying, discrimination, extremist propaganda or content targeting a person or group
  • Illegal activity, scams, fraud, deception, doxxing, threats or material designed to harm someone
  • Non-consensual, private, leaked or intimate footage or images
  • Content that impersonates someone, spreads harmful misinformation or is intended to mislead people
  • Anything I reasonably believe could be unsafe, illegal, unethical or damaging to another person

I may also decline projects that do not suit my current skills, schedule or creative direction.

How do we get started?

Use the Request a Quote form and include what you want edited, your deadline, the platform it is for and links to any video references you like. I’ll review the project and reply with any questions, availability and a clear quote if it is a suitable fit.
